# Predicting-Quality-of-Life-Index
Predicting Quality of Life Index: Comparative XGBoost–LSTM Study with SHAP-Based Explainable AI Replication GRU Extension Study | Under Dr. Thirumulanathan Dhayaparan, IIT Kanpur

• Replicated and enhanced a published study on predicting the Quality of Life Index using data from 88 countries (2016–2025). Achieved
an XGBoost R² of 0.9086, surpassing the paper’s 0.898, while also reducing prediction errors (MAE: 7.65, RMSE: 10.04).

• Trained deep learning architectures including LSTM and GRU for sequential prediction tasks. The LSTM model delivered the highest
overall accuracy (R² = 0.9503, MAE = 5.92). GRU extension achieved R² = 0.9378, outperforming XGBoost

• Applied SHAP Explainable AI to identify key drivers of Quality of Life index; top factors included Purchasing Power and Pollution.
